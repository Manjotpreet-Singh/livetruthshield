Challenge & Market Need

The Epidemic of Online Video Misinformation

Video is the world's primary communication medium, but fact-checking has remained painfully slow.
🚨
Virality Outpaces Truth
False narratives diffuse 6x faster than reality on video platforms. By the time manual fact-checking articles publish 48 hours later, millions have already internalized falsehoods.
🧩
The Multi-Claim Trap
Popular video essays mix 90% truth with 10% dangerous distortions (health pseudoscience, doctored numbers, misleading history). Viewers cannot spot the subtle fabrications.
🎙️
The Subtitle Phonetic Glitch
Automated speech recognition frequently generates phonetic homophones (e.g. transcribing 'Seoul' as 'Soul'). Primitive AI systems hallucinate falsehoods based on phonetic typos.
